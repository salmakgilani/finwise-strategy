# The Solution · FinWise

> Module 2 · Acquisition & Activation. The onboarding solution and the Aha moment that makes value land.

## Aha moment

The first time a trial user gets a cash answer from their own data, like "Yes, Friday's payroll is covered."

_____

## Onboarding prototype

[Clickable onboarding prototype](https://claude.ai/artifact/NQL1KHBo1K1i2e4mPmoanA), 4 screens from sign-up to Aha:

1. **What do you need to know?** Pick one question (payroll, hiring, or runway).
2. **Connect your money.** Connect bank or accounting software.
3. **Check what we found.** Confirm the pre-filled recurring items.
4. **Your answer.** See the answer, then set a Monday review.

![FinWise onboarding flow: 4 screens from sign-up to Aha](onboarding-flow.png)

_____

## Why this activates

Users end their first session with an answer to a question they picked, instead of an empty dashboard, and pre-filled data means they confirm instead of type.

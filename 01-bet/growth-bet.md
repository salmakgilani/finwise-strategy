# The Bet · FinWise

> Module 1 · Ignite a PLG Motion. The growth hypothesis and where you're betting, plus the growth-loop visualization.

## Growth hypothesis

FinWise's biggest growth problem is that trial-to-paid conversion is stuck at ~2%, because using the product in the trial isn't turning into a reason to pay.

_Working notes: First hunch was users don't see the value and conversion was dropping. Evidence: 98% of trials don't convert, and the rate stayed at 1.87–2.08% every month. Against the data: conversion didn't drop, it's flat, and import and modeling usage went up without moving it._

_____

## The bet

Trial → paid: get trial users to the action that predicts paying in their first week, to lift conversion from 2% to 3%.

**Not doing:** pricing changes, more acquisition spend, new premium features.

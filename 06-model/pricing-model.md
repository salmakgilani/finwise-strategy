## Recommendation

### Memo

**To:** FinWise Leadership Team
**From:** Senior PM, Growth
**Re:** Pricing recommendation, moving from trial-only to freemium

**The problem**
FinWise has product-market fit and $10M ARR, but our monetization isn't working. Only 2% of trial users convert to paid, and only 40% of paid users are still with us after a year. Trial-to-paid has stayed flat at 1.87–2.08% for 13 months, even though modeling usage rose to 58%. At the same time, average session time dropped from 12.1 to 5.1 minutes. Users are trying the product but not getting enough value to pay, so we keep paying to acquire new users who leave.

**Recommendation**
Keep the reverse trial for new trial users. When it ends, move them to a free tier instead of losing them:
- **Free:** cash today, from their own connected data
- **Paid:** the weekly cash forecast that answers "can I make payroll?" and "can I afford this hire?"

**Why now**
We're in Stage 1 (Value Creation). Value isn't proven yet, so cutting or raising prices won't fix the problem. Today, 98% of trial users leave with nothing. A free tier keeps them in the product until they need the forecast, and the paywall sits right at that moment.

**How we'll measure it**
The free tier only works with the user's own data, so it pushes users to import early. We'll track **% of trial signups who import data in session 1** as the leading indicator, and trial-to-paid conversion (target 2% → 3%) as the outcome.

**The bet:** packaging matched to value. The free/paid line sits where users feel they need more.

_____

## Model signal

Conversion stayed flat while usage went up, so value isn't proven yet and the paywall should sit where users want the forecast answer.
